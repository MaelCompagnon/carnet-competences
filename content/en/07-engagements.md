# My volunteering

## Why I get involved

My volunteering has changed my view of the engineering profession: technology must first serve people. I like collective projects, meeting people from very different backgrounds, and seeing the concrete effect of what I do, even in a one-hour class.

## Club ANT: Digital Accessibility for All

**Role:** co-president with Arthur Marie, then active member after the handover
**Period:** from September 2024 to September 2026, then active member until today
**Location:** municipal social services (CCAS) of Saint-Barthélemy-d'Anjou, every Thursday afternoon
**Audience:** about fifty seniors helped towards digital skills
**Time invested:** 3 hours per week in the field, plus preparation

### The context

ANT fights the digital divide, particularly among seniors. When we took it over in September 2024, the club was in transition: it had to be restructured before it could be developed.

### What we did

- **Two levels of classes** (beginner, then advanced) and a drop-in "Quick help" slot, with no registration, for specific questions.
- **Completely redone materials.** A survey of members let us add new themes: online administrative procedures, scam prevention, artificial intelligence.
- **Running the club:** schedules, internal tools, posters for the CCAS, coordination of volunteers.
- **An expansion towards Trélazé.** We approached several municipalities, then laid the groundwork for a partnership with the CCAS of Trélazé. The opening was delayed for administrative reasons.
- **Visibility:** an article in the local newspaper, participation in a conference on cybersecurity and AI, and two promotional shoots with ESAIP.
- **A prepared handover.** Before we left for Erasmus, we recruited the successors and left them a "toolbox": the history of decisions, the materials and a template schedule.

### Difficulties and successes

The hardest part was finding themes that were useful, accessible and motivating at once, and finding students willing to take over the club. My greatest success remains the trust built with the seniors: seeing them progress from session to session gives this commitment all its meaning.

## ESAIP student union (BDE): logistics manager

**Period:** 2024 – September 2026
**Duties:** material and technical logistics for the major student-life events: parties, freshers' weekend (WEI, more than 180 participants, 3 days of full commitment), Christmas gala. Managing stock, schedules and merchandising.

### The situation that stayed with me

I had ordered the WEI goodies two months in advance. Yet a blockage at the supplier threatened their delivery on the day. I urgently reorganised the delivery with the carrier so the boxes would be dropped directly at the campsite rather than at the school. The participants got their packs on time.
