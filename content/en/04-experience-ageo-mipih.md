# AGEO & MIPIH: my first steps in a company

**Type:** 1st-year internship of the integrated preparatory cycle (4-week immersion internship in two companies)
**Companies and dates:**
- **AGEO**, personal insurance broker, Châlons-en-Champagne: 29 January to 2 February 2024 (1 week). The group was bought by Howden in 2024, then renamed Howden in 2026.
- **MIPIH**, public player in digital health, Reims: 5 to 23 February 2024 (3 weeks). MIPIH merged with SIB to become Numih France in 2025.

## Why this choice

For my first internship, I wanted companies with an IT department large enough to discover several jobs: development, infrastructure, support, cybersecurity. I contacted three companies. One turned me down, and I got the other two for complementary periods: a real opportunity to compare two environments.

## What I did at AGEO

On the first day, I understood that spending a week watching developers code in languages I did not know would not teach me much. So I made two choices:
1. **Discover all the departments** (HR, accounting, management, secretarial, support…), observing how each uses the software developed in-house. I understood that all departments depend on one another, and on IT.
2. **Ask for real projects.**

**Development project.** Working from a mock-up by the marketing department, I built the password reset email and page for the website of SG Santé, a subsidiary of the group, in HTML and CSS, with responsive variants. I did not know how to code: I took an online course in the evenings to learn the basics, then moved forward with the developers' help.

**Security project.** An external cybersecurity audit (Stoïk) had given the information system a very low score, and the file had been waiting since July 2023. I listed and documented in a table the 24 critical risks identified: category, severity, description, scope, proposed solution and, where needed, a PowerShell verification command. The support team was able to start the work on a clear basis. For the fix itself in Active Directory, I stayed an observer: a single mistake could affect the whole infrastructure.

## What I did at MIPIH

For three weeks, I was embedded in a development team on a project, at the Reims premises. MIPIH (now Numih France) is a public body that develops and hosts software for many healthcare institutions, with high certification requirements (ISO 27001, health data hosting). There I discovered a different scale from AGEO: software used by hospitals across France, where security and data quality are non-negotiable.

## The difficulties

- **Starting from zero:** learning the basics of HTML and CSS in a few evenings to deliver a project intended for a subsidiary's official website.
- **Finding my place:** turning an observation internship into one where I produce something.
- **Knowing where to stop:** on Active Directory, understanding that a beginner must observe before touching.

## What I take from it

| Skill | How I developed it |
|---|---|
| Web development | HTML, CSS, responsive design, following a mock-up to the pixel |
| Cybersecurity and systems | Reading an audit, Active Directory, PowerShell |
| Autonomy and learning | Learning on my own, in the evenings, what I lacked to deliver |
| Business awareness | Understanding the interdependence of departments |
| Initiative | Asking for projects rather than staying a spectator |

## The link with my target role

This internship confirmed my choice of studies. It showed me that IT is not a separate department: it is what holds all the others together. It also taught me a rule I still apply: when you do not know, you learn fast, and you ask before touching anything that could break everything.
