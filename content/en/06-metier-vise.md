# My target role

## AI engineer for robotics

My goal: to design the systems that let robots perceive their environment and adapt to it. This role sits at the crossroads of three specialisms:
- **AI / machine learning engineer:** training, evaluating and improving models.
- **Computer vision engineer:** detecting, segmenting and locating objects from images and 3D sensors.
- **Embedded AI engineer for robotics:** running these models in real time, on the robot itself.

**Sector:** industrial and mobile robotics. **Where:** internationally, in any type of organisation: large groups (ABB, Bosch…), applied research institutes (Fraunhofer…) or robotics startups.

## The profile of the role

Built from an analysis of recent internship offers in AI for robotics and computer vision: ABB Robotics (United States and Germany), Fraunhofer IPK (Germany), Simbe Robotics and Nexteer (United States).

| Technical skills | Behavioural skills | Transferable skills |
|---|---|---|
| Solid Python (beyond notebooks), C++ | Solving ill-defined or novel problems | Communicating results and choices clearly |
| PyTorch or TensorFlow | Curiosity and fast learning | Working with software, hardware and data teams |
| Computer vision: detection, segmentation, pose estimation, OCR | Experimental rigour: measuring rather than assuming | Fluent English, and German appreciated in Germany |
| ROS 2, robotics simulation (Gazebo, Isaac Sim, RobotStudio) | Openness to feedback | Presenting a project to an audience |
| Preparing, cleaning and evaluating datasets, analysing errors | Autonomy on a defined project | Project management |
